Kilkari
Everything for Avika — an offline-first Android baby tracker. Interactive prototype, 16 screens.
Assumptions
· Single parent user, no account; data lives on-device with backup/export.
· Avika, girl, born 20 Aug 2026 — mockup date 15 Sep 2026 (3 wk 5 d).
· Vaccination schedule auto-generated from DOB; parent picks IAP (India private), UIP (India govt), WHO or CDC. Marking a dose done logs a life event and can post a medical expense.
· Photos stay in Google Photos; the app only stores album links.
· Documents are camera scans filed by date; export as PDF / share.
· Currency is a setting (₹ default); change it under More → Settings.
Design reasoning
· Five-tab bar: Today · Log · Health · Money · More. Vaccines/growth/teeth/meds/appointments cluster under Health; both expense types under Money.
· Quick logging is widget tiles showing the last entry time — the glanceable "when did she last feed?" is the most frequent question.
· Timeline (More) holds milestones, events and photo albums only; routine logs and money stay in their tabs.
· Warm, playful copy; indigo → fuchsia brand ramp, Bricolage Grotesque for display, Plus Jakarta Sans for UI.
Jump to a screen
Try it
· Health → Vaccines → 6 weeks → Mark all given: adds a Money entry and a Timeline event.
· Log → tap Feed to log a feed; the tile updates.
· Money → + adds an expense; Settings changes currency everywhere.
Next: real photo/scan assets, notification designs, onboarding (DOB + schedule picker), multi-baby support.
